1. What WellChapter is

WellChapter (previously Silent Dose), by Ozotea Software (OPC) Pvt. Ltd., is a personal organiser for medicines, doses and stock, doctor visits and hospital admissions, vaccinations, health records and documents, vitals and lab results, payments and your health journey, for you and the family members you add. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always follow the instructions of a qualified healthcare professional, and never change a prescribed course based on what the app shows.

2. Not for emergencies

Do not rely on WellChapter in a medical emergency. Contact your local emergency services or your doctor directly.

3. Reminders are best-effort

Reminders, alarms and low-stock alerts are scheduled by your device, so they depend on it. Battery optimisation, Do Not Disturb or Focus, silent mode, denied notification or exact-alarm permissions, force-stopping the app, a powered-off device, or a change of device or time zone can all delay or prevent an alert. You remain responsible for taking your medicines and attending your visits; WellChapter is not liable for a missed dose, refill or appointment.

4. Healthy ranges are guides, not diagnoses

Vitals and lab results are coloured against typical adult reference ranges to help you read them. Ranges differ by age, sex, lab and condition, and a value marked Normal, Borderline, High or Low is not a diagnosis. Discuss every result with your doctor.

5. Information you enter or the app fills in

You are responsible for the accuracy of what you record. Some details can be filled in for you, such as a medicine’s name and type from a photo of its pack, a lot number from a barcode, or an appointment found in an SMS from a hospital. These are best-effort conveniences that can misread a name, strength, date or time, so always check them before saving. If you record data about another person, including a child or a dependent adult, you confirm you are entitled to do so.

6. Your data and your backups

Your records are stored on your device. Keeping your device secure, with a screen lock and the app’s optional App Lock, is your responsibility. Exported backup files and any copy kept in your Google Drive are under your control once created, as is anything you share out of the app, such as a PDF report, an Emergency Health Card image or a message to a pharmacy. Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage or losing the device removes local data permanently, and we have no copy to restore from.
