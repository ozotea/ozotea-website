WellChapter

A private medicine reminder and family health organiser. Every dose, visit and record, in one calm place.

What it does

WellChapter keeps your family’s health in one calm place: medicines and doses, doctor visits and hospital stays, vaccinations, health records and payments. It is private and offline-first: everything stays on your device, with optional backup to your own Google Drive. No ads, no tracking, and no account with us.

  • Medicine reminders for any schedule, with Taken, Skipped and Snoozed doses and stock and refill alerts
  • Doctor visits, bookings and hospital admissions, with reminders, notes and directions
  • Vaccinations, vitals and lab results, and scanned health records for every family member
  • Emergency Health Card with a QR code first responders can scan
  • Wear OS and Apple Watch apps for reminders on your wrist
  • Optional backup to a “WellChapter” folder in your own Google Drive

Get WellChapter

Get it on Google Play

Available on Android phones, tablets and Wear OS. iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch versions are on the way. WellChapter was previously called Silent Dose.

How WellChapter uses your Google account

Signing in with Google is optional and is used only to back up your WellChapter data to your own Google Drive and restore it on a new device. The app asks only for your email address and name and the drive.file permission, so it can see only the files it creates. Read the details in the Privacy Policy.
