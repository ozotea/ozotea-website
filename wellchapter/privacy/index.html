WellChapter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

WellChapter keeps your family’s medicines, doses, doctor visits, vaccinations and health records on your own device. There are no accounts of ours, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Backup to your own Google Drive is optional, and we never see your data.

Who we are

WellChapter is a private, offline-first medicine reminder and family health organiser for Android phones and tablets, Wear OS watches, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. It is developed by Ozotea Software (OPC) Pvt. Ltd. (“Ozotea”, “we”, “us”), Bengaluru, India. WellChapter was previously called Silent Dose. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept and the choices you have. Our website is covered by the separate Ozotea Privacy Policy.

What you record

Everything is optional, and the app works with as little as you want to enter. You may record:

  • Family profiles: name, relation, date of birth, gender, blood group, height, weight, conditions, allergies, emergency contact and an optional photo.
  • Medicines: name, form, dose, schedule, reminder times, course dates, stock, refills, notes and an optional photo, plus your dose history and any reason you give for a missed dose.
  • Doctor visits and hospital admissions: doctor, hospital or clinic, date and time, optional clinic address and map coordinates, notes, and prescriptions or reports you attach.
  • Vaccinations, vitals and lab results, health records with the documents, photos and links you attach, payments and bills, and health journey events.
  • Settings: theme, App Lock, currency, active profile and, if you sign in for Drive backup, that Google account’s email address and name.

Where it is kept

In a private on-device database and private app folders that other apps cannot read, protected by your device’s own encryption. The app works offline. We do not run a server and nothing you record is sent to us; we cannot see your data.

Google sign-in and Google Drive backup

Backup is optional and off until you sign in with Google under Profile → Backup & sync. Sign-in happens on Google’s own screen; WellChapter never sees your Google password.

  • What is accessed. Your Google account’s email address and name (shown in the app so you know which account is connected), and the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, which limits the app to files it created itself. It cannot see or read the rest of your Drive.
  • What is uploaded. Your backup file (a copy of your records) and the photos, certificates, documents and receipts those records reference, into a folder named “WellChapter” in your own Drive. Backups made before the rename sat in a folder called “SilentDose”; the app renames that folder in place so nothing is lost.
  • When. With Auto backup on, shortly after you change something while online, and whenever you tap “Back up now”. You can turn Auto backup off, and signing out stops uploads entirely.
  • Restore. The app never restores or overwrites your data on its own, and it will not overwrite a Drive backup that another of your devices made more recently.
  • Where it lives. In your Google account under Google’s Privacy Policy. Ozotea has no access to it.
  • Your controls. Sign out in the app, delete the “WellChapter” folder in Drive, or remove the app’s access in your Google Account settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy. WellChapter’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to sign you in and to back up and restore your own data in your own Drive. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to anyone, and not read by people.

SMS appointment detection (optional, Android)

If you allow SMS access, WellChapter reads messages from the last 30 days on your phone to spot appointment confirmations from hospitals and clinics and offer to add them to Bookings. Messages are read entirely on your device; none is uploaded or stored. Only an appointment you choose to add is saved, with a marker so the same message isn’t offered twice. Remove the SMS permission in Android settings at any time.

Maps, pharmacies and other services

  • Visit locations. Address search and map tiles come from OpenStreetMap and Nominatim on Android, and Apple Maps on Apple devices. Only the text you search or the coordinates you pick are sent, never your name or health records.
  • Nearby pharmacies. When you tap “Buy medicine nearby”, your current coordinates are sent to TomTom (Android) or Apple Maps (iOS) to find pharmacies close by. Results are not stored. Messaging a pharmacy opens WhatsApp with what you choose to send.
  • Directions. “Get directions” hands the address to your maps app, which applies its own privacy policy.

We are not affiliated with OpenStreetMap, TomTom, Apple or Google.

Camera, scanning and text recognition

The camera is used only when you take a photo, scan a document or scan a barcode. Text and barcode recognition run on your device (Google ML Kit on Android, Apple Vision on Apple devices); no image or scanned text is uploaded. Google may receive basic, anonymous diagnostic information about how ML Kit performs, under Google’s terms.

Emergency Health Card

The card’s QR code carries a profile’s name, blood group, allergies, conditions and emergency contact inside the link itself, after the “#” of https://ozotea.github.io/silentdose-emergency/. Whoever scans it opens a page that reads those details on their own phone; browsers never send that part of a link to the website, and nothing is stored on any server. If you put the card on your lock screen, anyone holding your phone can scan it; that is the point in an emergency.

Watches and Apple Health

If you use WellChapter on a paired Wear OS watch or Apple Watch, your phone sends it what it needs to show your reminders, such as upcoming doses and medicine names, directly over the connection between your devices. Nothing passes through us.

On iPhone, if you allow it, WellChapter reads your step count and heart rate from Apple Health to show simple trends. Access is read-only, the data stays on your device and is never included in backups or sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alerts, vibration, wake lock, run-on-boot: to deliver reminders reliably and restore them after a restart.
  • Camera (optional): photos and scanning.
  • Location (optional): a single reading to centre the map picker or find pharmacies nearby. No background tracking.
  • SMS (optional, Android): appointment detection, above.
  • Wallpaper (optional, Android): putting the Emergency Health Card on the lock screen.
  • Contacts (optional, iOS): the system picker returns only the one emergency contact you choose.
  • Biometrics (optional): App Lock only, handled by the system. No biometric data reaches the app.
  • Internet: the optional features above.

You can decline any optional permission and still use the core app.

What we never do

No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking. No selling or sharing of your data. No access to your contacts, microphone or files beyond the ones you pick. WellChapter does not read Health Connect, wearables or fitness services on Android.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Delete medicines, visits, vaccinations, vitals, records, payments, events or whole family members inside the app.
  • Uninstalling, or clearing the app’s storage, removes everything held locally.
  • If you used Drive backup, delete the “WellChapter” folder in your Drive as well.
  • Exported backup files and PDF reports are ordinary files under your control.
  • Because your data never reaches us, there is nothing on our side to delete.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data and withdraw consent. As your data stays on your device and in your own Drive, you can do almost all of this in the app; for anything else, contact us below.

Children and family members

WellChapter is intended for adults, who may also manage health records for family members including children. Please only record another person’s health details if you are entitled to do so. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and no data is transmitted to us.

Security

Data sits in the app’s private, sandboxed storage, protected by your device’s encryption. Turn on App Lock for another layer, and keep a screen lock on your device. Connections to Google and map services use HTTPS.

Changes to this policy

If the way WellChapter handles information changes, we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date above. The same policy is available in the app under Profile → Privacy Policy.
