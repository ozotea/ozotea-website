Jazba Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Jazba tracks your badminton games, matches, scores and players. It works on your phone first. If you sign in with Google, your data syncs through Firebase so you can use it on several devices and share live games with the people you invite. Backup to your own Google Drive is optional. There are no ads and no analytics.

Who we are

Jazba (“Every Game. Every Victory.”) is a badminton game, match, score and player-performance tracker. It is developed by Ozotea Software (OPC) Pvt. Ltd. (“Ozotea”, “we”, “us”), Bengaluru, India. This policy explains what information the Jazba app handles, where it is kept and the choices you have. Our website is covered by the separate Ozotea Privacy Policy.

Data on your device

Jazba stores the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • Players you add: name, skill level, optional photo you pick, and whether a player is you.
  • Games: name, date, scheduled and actual times, pauses, location, court number, notes and the players taking part (up to six).
  • Matches: format, teams, scores, winner, start and end times, pauses and notes.
  • Settings: theme, match and reminder preferences, and the last time data was synced or backed up.

Player photos stay on your device; only a reference to the photo is kept with the player record and the image itself is never uploaded. Other apps can’t read Jazba’s storage, and Jazba excludes its data from Android’s automatic cloud backup.

Guest mode and Google sign-in

You can explore Jazba as a guest without an account. Guests can browse the app but can’t create or change anything, and nothing is uploaded.

If you choose Continue with Google, sign-in is handled by Google and Firebase Authentication. Jazba never sees your Google password. Firebase gives the app a user ID and your Google account’s name, email address and profile photo link. Jazba keeps these in a private profile record (name, email, photo link, account creation and last sign-in time) that only you can read.

Cloud sync with Firebase

When you are signed in, your players, games and matches are stored in Cloud Firestore, a Google Firebase service, so they stay in sync across your devices and can be restored after you reinstall. Changes are sent as soon as you make them and received live while the app is open.

  • Your player directory is private to your account.
  • A game is visible only to its creator and the people they invite (see below).
  • Access is enforced by security rules on Google’s servers, not just by the app.

Google processes this data on our behalf as a service provider under the Firebase data processing terms. Data is stored in Google Cloud data centres and may be processed outside India.

Shared games and invites

A game’s creator can invite others to follow it live. To invite someone, the creator enters that person’s email address and can tag them as one of the game’s players (for example “Sahid”). The invite stores the invitee’s email, the tagged player’s name, the game name and the creator’s name, and expires after 14 days. It is deleted when the invitee joins or declines.

Everyone in a shared game can see its name, date, times, location, court, notes, the creator’s name, the players’ names and skill levels, and all matches and scores in it, including matches recorded before they joined. When an invitee joins as a tagged player, their account is linked to that player so the app can show “your” record.

The creator controls the game. People invited to update points may only change the score of a match in progress; people invited to watch can’t change anything. Only invite people who expect to receive an invite, and only share information you’re comfortable sharing with everyone in the game.

Invites are sent the way you choose (for example WhatsApp, SMS or email) through your phone’s share sheet. Jazba does not send messages on your behalf.

Google Drive backup

What is accessed. Backup is optional. Jazba requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata permission, which gives it a hidden folder in your Drive that only Jazba can use. Jazba can’t see any of your other Drive files.

What is uploaded. One file, jazba-backup.json, containing your players, games, matches and settings. It is created or replaced only when you tap Back up to Google Drive, and read only when you choose to restore. Before restoring, the app shows what will change. Ozotea has no access to your Drive or to this file.

Tokens. Access tokens are short-lived and kept only in memory.

Your controls. You can remove Jazba’s access at any time in your Google Account settings. Hidden app data can be deleted in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.

Google API Services User Data Policy. Jazba’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to sign you in, sync your data and provide backup and restore. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to others except as needed to provide these features, and not read by people unless you ask us to help with a problem.

Booking messages you paste

With Booked a court? you can paste a court-booking confirmation (for example from a booking app). The text is read on your device to fill in the game’s name, date, time, location and court. The pasted message itself is not uploaded or stored; only the game details you save are, along with any RSVP link added to the game notes. Jazba reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste from clipboard.

Who we share data with

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Drive) to sign you in, sync, share and back up your data, as described above.
  • People you invite to a game, who see that game as described in “Shared games and invites”.

We do not sell or rent your data, and Jazba contains no advertising or analytics libraries. We may disclose information if required by law. Like any internet request, Google’s services can see your device’s IP address; Google Play services may also collect limited diagnostic information as described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to sign in, sync, share live games and back up, and to sync only when you’re online.
  • Notifications (optional): game reminders and a nudge if a live match is left running. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Player photos are chosen with Android’s photo picker, so Jazba receives only the images you pick. Jazba does not request camera, contacts, location or storage permissions.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it, sign out or uninstall the app.
  • Sign out first syncs your changes, then removes your Jazba data from that device. It stays in your account and returns when you sign in again.
  • Delete a game you created (open it, or use ⋮ in History) to permanently remove it and all its matches and open invites from your device and our cloud. It also disappears for everyone you shared it with, and if you use Google Drive backup, your backup is rewritten without it.
  • Leave a game shared with you to stop seeing it and remove it from your device.
  • Delete account (Profile → Account) permanently deletes your Jazba account, your profile, your player directory, every game you created with its matches, and your pending invites, from our cloud and from your device. Your Google Drive backup is not deleted; you control it in Google Drive.
  • Games created by someone else stay with their creator after you delete your account. To have your name or link removed from them, contact us.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the time required by law.

Security

Data on your device is protected by Android’s app sandbox and your device’s encryption. Data in the cloud is protected by Firebase security rules that allow each account to access only what it is entitled to, and all network traffic uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so we recommend using a screen lock on your phone.

Children

Jazba is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Where the law requires it, a parent or guardian should manage the account of a child who plays.

Changes to this policy

If the way Jazba handles information changes, we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app or its store listing.
