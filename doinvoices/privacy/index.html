DoInvoices Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who we are

DoInvoices is an app for creating invoices, tracking payments and issuing receipts. It is developed by Ozotea Software (OPC) Pvt. Ltd. (“Ozotea”, “we”, “us”), Bengaluru, India. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept and the choices you have. Our website is covered by the separate Ozotea Privacy Policy.

In short: your data stays on your device, Google sign-in is optional and backs up only to your own Drive, and the app has no ads or analytics. We do not run servers that store your invoices.

Data on your device

When you use DoInvoices, the app stores the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • Your businesses: name, email, address, tax and registration numbers, default tax rates, and the logo, signature and payment QR code images you add.
  • Your customers: name, email, address, currency and logo.
  • Your item catalogue: names, descriptions, prices, quantities and any barcode you scanned.
  • Invoices, payments and receipts: line items, taxes, discounts, dates, payment method, references and notes.
  • App settings, such as theme, display currency, invoice design and, if you sign in, your Google account’s name and email address.
  • Exchange rates, cached so currency conversion works offline.

Invoice and receipt PDFs are drawn when you view, print or share them. They are kept only briefly, so you can hand them to another app, and are removed when the app next starts.

Other apps can’t read this storage. DoInvoices excludes its data from Android’s automatic cloud backup, so your data only leaves the device in the ways described here.

Guest use and Google sign-in

You can use DoInvoices as a guest without any account. Guest data stays on your device only.

If you choose Continue with Google, sign-in is handled by Google Play services. DoInvoices never sees your Google password. After you grant access, the app reads your account’s name and email address from Google Drive to show which account is connected. Your name and email are kept on your device and are not sent to Ozotea.

Google Drive backup and sync

What is accessed. DoInvoices requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. It lets the app create files in your Drive and see or change only the files it created. It cannot see your other Drive files.

What is uploaded. One backup file, DoInvoices Backup.json, in a “DoInvoices” folder in your Drive. It holds the records listed under “Data on your device” together with your logo, signature and payment QR images. PDFs are not uploaded. While you are signed in, the backup is updated automatically shortly after you make changes, and you can also back up or restore from Settings. Google stores the file under the terms of your Google account. Ozotea has no access to your Drive or to this file.

Sync with the iPhone app. The DoInvoices iPhone app uses the same backup file, so signing in with the same Google account on both lets them share your data.

Tokens. Access tokens are short-lived and kept only in memory.

Your controls. You can sign out at any time in Settings. You can also remove DoInvoices’ access in your Google Account settings. The backup file stays in your Drive until you delete it in Google Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy. DoInvoices’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google Drive is used only to provide backup, restore and sync inside the app. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to others and not read by people.

Online lookups

A few features send small, non-personal requests to outside services. None of them receives your invoices, customers or account details.

  • Exchange rates. The app downloads daily currency rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). The request contains no information about you.
  • Barcode scanning. The scanner is provided by Google Play services, so DoInvoices does not ask for camera permission. To fill in a product name, the scanned barcode number is looked up in Open Food Facts, Open Products Facts and UPCitemdb. Only the barcode number is sent.
  • Address autofill. When you enter a PIN or ZIP code, Android’s geocoding service, provided by Google, looks up the city, state and country. Only the postal code you typed is sent. The app does not use your location.

Like any internet request, these services can see your device’s IP address. Google Play services may also collect limited diagnostic information, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Sharing

DoInvoices does not share your information with anyone. An invoice or receipt leaves the app only when you choose to share or print it, for example by sending a PDF through WhatsApp or email. The app contains no advertising or analytics libraries, and we do not sell or rent data.

Permissions

  • Internet: for Google Drive backup and sync, exchange rates and product lookups.

Logos, signatures and QR images are picked with Android’s photo picker, so DoInvoices receives only the images you choose. The app does not request camera, contacts, location or storage permissions.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Your data stays on your device until you delete it.
  • Deleted invoices go to Trash and are removed for good after 30 days, or sooner if you delete them there.
  • Signing out of a Google account removes that account’s data from the device. It remains in your Drive backup.
  • Uninstalling DoInvoices removes all data the app stored on your device. Your Drive backup remains until you delete it in Google Drive.

Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox and your device’s encryption. All network requests, including transfers to and from Google Drive, use HTTPS. DoInvoices does not currently add its own extra encryption layer to data on your device, so we recommend using a screen lock on your phone.

Children

DoInvoices is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the way DoInvoices handles information changes, we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app or its store listing.
