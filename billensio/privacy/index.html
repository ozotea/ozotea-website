Billensio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who we are

Billensio is an Android app for saving and organising bills, receipts, invoices and warranty documents. It is developed by Ozotea Software (OPC) Pvt. Ltd. (“Ozotea”, “we”, “us”), Bengaluru, India. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept and the choices you have. Our website is covered by the separate Ozotea Privacy Policy.

In short: your documents stay on your device, text on them is read on your device, Google Drive backup is optional and goes to your own Drive, and the app has no ads or analytics. We do not run servers that store your documents.

Data on your device

When you use Billensio, the app stores the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • Images and PDF files of documents you scan, import from your gallery or import as PDFs.
  • Details you enter or confirm, such as store name, purchase date, amounts, tax, invoice number, products, brand, model, serial numbers, warranty periods, return dates, notes, tags and collections.
  • Text recognised from your documents, kept so you can search your bills offline.
  • Reminders and app settings, such as theme, default currency and backup preferences.

Other apps can’t read this storage. Billensio excludes its data from Android’s automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your documents only leave the device in the ways described here.

Scanning and text recognition

Text recognition uses Google ML Kit with a model built into the app. It runs entirely on your device, and your document images are not uploaded for it.

The camera scanner is provided by Google Play services (ML Kit Document Scanner). Billensio does not ask for camera permission: the scanner runs in Google Play services and hands the finished scan to Billensio. Google Play services may download the scanner component and may collect limited diagnostic and usage information about these features, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Picking photos and PDFs uses Android’s system pickers. Billensio receives only the files you choose and does not ask for access to your whole photo library or storage.

Google Drive backup

Backup is off until you choose Connect Google Drive in Settings, confirm, and grant access on Google’s own sign-in screen.

What is accessed. Billensio requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. It lets the app create files in your Drive and see or change only the files it created. It cannot see your other Drive files.

What is uploaded. Copies of your document files and a backup file (billensio-backup.json) with the details listed under “Data on your device”, placed in a “Billensio” folder in your Drive. Google stores them under the terms of your Google account. Ozotea has no access to your Drive or to these files.

Sign-in and tokens. Sign-in is handled by Google Play services. Billensio never sees your Google password and keeps access tokens only in memory while a backup or restore runs. The app reads your account’s email address from Google Drive so it can show which account is connected.

Restore. On a new device, Billensio can read this backup from your Drive to restore your documents. Each file is checked against the fingerprint recorded at backup time before it is saved.

Your controls. You can turn off automatic backup, limit backup to Wi-Fi or disconnect at any time in Settings. Disconnecting stops backup and removes Billensio’s access to your Drive. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them in Google Drive. You can also remove Billensio’s access in your Google Account settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy. Billensio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google Drive is used only to provide backup and restore inside the app. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to others and not read by people.

Sharing

Billensio does not share your information with anyone. A document leaves the app only when you choose to share it or save a copy, for example by sending a PDF to another app or saving a file to a folder you pick. The app contains no advertising or analytics libraries, and we do not sell or rent data.

Reminders

Warranty, return and service reminders are calculated and scheduled on your device. Notifications appear only if you allow them, and you can turn reminders off in Settings at any time.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): to show reminders.
  • Internet and network state: for Google Drive backup and restore, and so Google Play services can provide the scanner.
  • Background work and start after restart: so backups and reminders can be scheduled with Android’s WorkManager.

Billensio does not request camera, contacts, location or broad storage permissions.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Your documents stay on your device until you delete them.
  • Deleted documents go to Recently deleted and are removed for good after 30 days, or sooner if you delete them there.
  • When a document is removed for good and backup is on, its files in your Drive are moved to the Drive trash on the next backup.
  • Uninstalling Billensio removes all data the app stored on your device. Copies in your Google Drive remain until you delete them.

Security

Your documents are kept in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox and your device’s encryption. Transfers to and from Google Drive use HTTPS. Billensio does not currently add its own extra encryption layer to files on your device, so we recommend using a screen lock on your phone.

Children

Billensio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the way Billensio handles information changes, we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app or its store listing.
