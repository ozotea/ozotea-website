What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website can save in your browser to remember things about you, such as whether you’re signed in or which pages you visited. Similar tools, like local storage, work the same way.

Cookies on this website

This website does not set any cookies. We don’t use advertising or cross-site tracking tools, and we don’t build a profile of you.

To understand how many people visit and from which countries, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It works without cookies and without fingerprinting: it counts page views and records the page, the referring site, the browser and device type, and the country (worked out from your IP address, which is not stored).

The one thing we remember

When you accept the cookie notice, we save a single setting in your browser’s local storage so the notice doesn’t appear again:

  • Name: oz_cookie_consent
  • Value: the date you accepted
  • Purpose: hide the notice on later visits
  • Where it’s kept: only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and contains no personal data.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts supplies the site’s typefaces. It doesn’t set cookies, but your browser sends your IP address to Google when the fonts load.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits without cookies, as described above.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep standard server logs for security. It doesn’t set cookies on this site.
  • External links to the App Store, Google Play, Facebook, Instagram, Dribbble and our clients’ sites take you to services that use their own cookies under their own policies.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. Clearing this site’s data also removes the setting above, so the notice will show again on your next visit.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add tools that use cookies, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent before any non-essential cookies are set.
